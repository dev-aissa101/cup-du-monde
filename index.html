About the tournament

A world first: three hosts, two continents

The 2030 FIFA World Cup will be the 24th edition of the quadrennial international tournament contested by the men's national teams of FIFA's member associations. For the first time, three countries from two continents will host the competition: Morocco, Portugal and Spain.

Argentina, Paraguay and Uruguay will also host matches to commemorate the 100th anniversary of the World Cup, in particular the first tournament in Uruguay. The opening game, alongside a special centenary celebration, will be held at the Estadio Centenario in Montevideo.

The second and third games will take place in Argentina and Paraguay respectively. The rest of the tournament will be played in Spain, Morocco and Portugal.

The venues

Stadiums across three hosts

Before finalizing the bid book on 31 July 2024, the Royal Spanish Football Federation announced 11 proposed stadiums in 9 cities. Two more venues — Nou Mestalla in Valencia and Balaídos in Vigo — were also considered, but including them would have exceeded FIFA's maximum of twenty stadiums.

The host city list was finalized 12 days later: six stadiums in six cities in Morocco, three in two cities in Portugal and eleven in nine cities in Spain — twenty stadiums in seventeen cities in total.
